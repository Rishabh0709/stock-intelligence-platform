# Product Vision

## Stock Intelligence Platform

An intelligent investment platform designed to help long-term investors make better investment decisions through data-driven analysis, portfolio analytics and explainable AI.

---

# Problem Statement

Retail investors today have access to enormous amounts of financial data.

However, they still struggle to answer simple questions such as:

- Why is my portfolio underperforming?
- Which stock contributed most to my returns?
- Is this company fundamentally improving?
- Is the sector becoming stronger?
- Should I buy more, hold or exit?

Most platforms provide information.

Very few provide actionable intelligence.

---

# Vision

Build India's most intelligent investment analysis platform.

Instead of simply showing data, explain:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- What it means
- What should be done next

---

# Target Users

Primary

- Long-term equity investors

Secondary

- Portfolio managers
- Financial advisors
- Students learning equity research

---

# Guiding Principles

Every feature must satisfy at least one of these goals.

✓ Improve investment decisions

✓ Save research time

✓ Reduce behavioural bias

✓ Improve portfolio quality

✓ Explain recommendations

If it doesn't help investors make better decisions, it doesn't belong in the product.

---

# Long Term Goals

• Complete Portfolio Intelligence

• Explainable Recommendation Engine

# Success Metrics

A successful platform should enable an investor to answer:

✓ Why?

✓ What changed?

✓ What should I do next?

within a few seconds.
