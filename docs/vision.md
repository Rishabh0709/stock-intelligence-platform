# Stock Intelligence Platform

## Vision

Build a world-class investment intelligence platform that enables investors to make evidence-based investment decisions.

Unlike existing platforms that specialize in only one aspect of investing, this platform consolidates market data, financial statements, valuation metrics, technical analysis, portfolio analytics, benchmarking, and AI-powered insights into one unified system.

The platform should answer questions such as:

- How healthy is my portfolio?
- Am I outperforming my benchmark?
- Which companies are fundamentally improving?
- Which stocks are overvalued or undervalued?
- Which companies have the highest probability of outperforming over the next 1–5 years?
- Why does the platform recommend Buy, Hold or Sell?

Core principles:

- Explainable recommendations
- Source-independent architecture
- Modular design
- Reproducible analytics
- Long-term maintainability
