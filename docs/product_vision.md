# Vision

Help an investor make better decisions in under 10 minutes every morning.

# Core Questions

- How healthy is my portfolio?
- Am I outperforming my benchmark?
- Which holdings require attention?
- Which companies have become fundamentally stronger?
- Which companies are overvalued?
- Should I Buy, Hold or Sell?
- Why is that the recommendation?

# Design Principle

Every page must answer an investment question.
